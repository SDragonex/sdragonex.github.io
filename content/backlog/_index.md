### 1. `2025-10-26` Variant Sudoku Solver
I spend a lot of time watching [Cracking The Cryptic](https://www.youtube.com/c/CrackingTheCryptic)
where Simon and Mark solve *variant sudokus*, [sudokus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sudoku)
that include more rules than the original 1 to 9
digits constraints on columns, rows and boxes, therefore I would like to explore
the idea of applying the constraint propagation method from the [Peter Norvig post](https://norvig.com/sudoku.html)
on solving sudoku with additional rules.
